Vektri's implementation uses bounded sequential CPU inference and sampled video
evidence. Its OpenCV expanded rotated rectangles replace upstream pyclipper
polygon unclip. It omits the orientation classifier, limits frame/crop sizes and
box/sample counts, and adds model validation, cancellation and RAM checks.

These are RapidAI conversions of official Paddle models, not official Paddle
ONNX exports. Upstream author attribution and Apache-2.0 terms are retained;
no author or project endorsement is implied.

Vektri modifications include optional bounded larger frames and overlapping
tiles, raw-reading and coordinate provenance, unrotated fallback crops,
sharpness-based nearby-frame selection, exact-text temporal links, and
model-specific embedded-dictionary validation. Its detection thresholds and
OpenCV rectangle expansion differ from official V5 defaults and polygon unclip.
The orientation classifier remains omitted. These changes do not establish
upstream performance parity or make recognized text trusted instructions.
